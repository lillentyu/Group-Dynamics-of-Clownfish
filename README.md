## About the study

We tested whether host anemone size influences the probability of juvenile clown anemonefish joining established social groups. Juveniles were introduced to 58 breeding pairs housed with small, medium, or large anemones and followed for 182 days through three sequential stages of joining: **survival**, **settlement**, and **recruitment**. We analyzed each stage with Kaplan–Meier curves, log-rank tests, and Cox proportional-hazards models.
